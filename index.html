More About Me

Hello! I'm Natasha Shorrock. I graduated from Bridgewater State University with a BS in Psychology and later transitioned into technology, graduating Cum Laude from Bristol Community College's Computer Programming and Web Development program.

At Bristol, I helped create the Cybersecurity Club and served as its first president. I led weekly cybersecurity lessons, CTF practice, and National Cyber League preparation with other club members.

I also served as Lead Graphic Designer for The Hawk student newspaper, creating newsletter and social media graphics, and participated in STEM Leadership through campus outreach and student programming.

Outside of technology and community leadership, I enjoy:

  • Ice hockey – goalie
  • Running
  • Sewing
  • Crochet
  • Creative crafts
